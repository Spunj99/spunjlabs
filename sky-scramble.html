Sky Scramble

Released

Take off, climb, and knock your friends out of the sky. Sky Scramble is a local-multiplayer arcade dogfighter for up to four players — momentum-driven flight where stalling is as dangerous as the guns behind you.

Get it on Steam Request a press key

Features

  • Four players, one screenCouch versus for up to four, controller-first from the ground up — plus Steam Remote Play Together for friends who aren't on the couch.
  • Four eras of flightBiplanes, WW2 fighters, jets and drones. Each era flies differently, with its own handling, weapons and silhouette.
  • Five hand-drawn levelsEngland, Italy, Egypt, Prussia and Japan — each with its own skyline, sky gradient and weather.
  • Twelve pilotsPick your pilot and colour in the lobby. Everyone gets a face, a name and a plane to lose.
  • Ejector seatsShot down over open sky? Bail out. Parachute down, draw your pistol, and fight your way back to the hangar on foot.
  • Bots that fight backShort on players? Fill the empty slots with bots that climb, dive, break off and barrel roll.
  • 18 Steam achievementsFrom First Blood to Legend — including a few you'll only get by doing something extremely unwise.
  • English & 简体中文Fully localised in English and Simplified Chinese, auto-detected on launch.
