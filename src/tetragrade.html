Tetragrade

In Development

A block-stacking roguelite carved from marble and gold. The falling-block game underneath is pure and unchanged — but every run banks coins, and every coin buys a favour from the gods that rewrites what the next run can do.

Follow development

What it is

  • The classic game, untouchedStandard pieces, standard scoring, standard speed curve. Upgrades stack on top of the game — they never quietly play it for you.
  • Coins as you clearCoins drop into the tray the instant lines vanish, not in a lump sum at the end. You feel the payout land.
  • The Temple of the GodsTen gods, ten upgrade paths — Gaia, Tyche, Midas, Plutus, Ares, Chronos, Aion, Epimetheus, Prometheus and Zeus.
  • Powers that break the rulesTurn your piece to gold, reforge it as a spear, rewind a bad placement, or call down lightning to erase the bottom four rows.
  • Permanent progressionUpgrades persist between runs, and gameplay milestones unlock new ones. Every run leaves you stronger than the last.
  • Marble and goldAn Ancient Greek art direction — carved stone, warm gold leaf and temple light, all the way through the UI.
  • Built for the DeckDesigned against Steam Deck Verified criteria from day one: full controller support and readable text at handheld size.
  • Leaderboards & achievementsSteam leaderboards and achievements are planned for launch, including a global score board with friend rankings.
