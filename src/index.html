About

SpunjLabs builds small games with a clear idea at the centre of them. No open worlds, no season passes. Just a controller, a few minutes, and something worth mastering.

Everything is made in Godot, designed controller-first, and tested on real hardware before it ships. If it doesn't feel right on a Steam Deck, it isn't finished.

How we work

  • Controller-first design, every game
  • Built and tested on Steam Deck
  • Released when it plays well, not when it's due
  • Supported after launch with real patches
